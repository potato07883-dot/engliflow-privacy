Privacy Policy for EngliFlow

Last updated: October 2026

No account needed

EngliFlow does not ask you to create an account or log in. We do not collect your name, email address or phone number.

Data stored on your device

Your learning progress (level, streak, accuracy and completed lessons) and your theme choice are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Learning content

Lessons, vocabulary, stories and practice questions are downloaded from our servers (Google Firebase) so the content can stay up to date. Like any internet service, Firebase may process technical data such as your IP address when the app downloads this content. We do not link it to you.

Ads

EngliFlow shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads. You can read how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/privacy, and you can reset or turn off personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

Children

EngliFlow is not directed at children under 13.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
